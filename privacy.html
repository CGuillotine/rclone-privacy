Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This application configuration is for the owner's personal use of rclone to access and manage files in their own Google Drive.

Data access and use

The application accesses Google Drive data only as authorised by the owner and only to perform the file listing, transfer, synchronisation and other file operations initiated by the owner.

Storage and sharing

This website does not collect or store Google Drive files, Google account passwords or OAuth tokens. rclone may store OAuth credentials locally in its configuration, and downloaded files may be stored on the owner's devices or other destinations selected by the owner.

Google Drive files may be copied to destinations explicitly configured by the owner. This application does not independently sell or share Google user data.

Security and contact

The owner is responsible for securing their devices, rclone configuration and any configured file destinations. For questions about this personal application, contact: cycloneflash@hotmail.com
